Getting started

Use Super El Nino to find community support, resources and preparation plans, or to share a need, offer or local update. Community information may be incomplete or unverified; check current guidance from local authorities before acting.

Need help?

For technical problems, feature requests or feedback, open an issue in the project repository. Do not include private contact details, health information, precise addresses or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Urgent situations

Super El Nino is not an emergency service. For an immediate threat to life, health or safety, contact local emergency services and the relevant public authorities.
