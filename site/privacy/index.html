Privacy

How to use the service with care.

Information you choose to share

Super El Nino is a community coordination service. Information you submit can include a contribution title, description, approximate location, category, availability and any contact method that you expressly choose to publish. Treat public contributions as visible to other people using the service.

Keep sensitive information out

Do not submit precise addresses, passwords, financial details, government identifiers, health information, information about children, or another person’s private information unless you have a clear lawful basis and their permission.

Connected services

When you use Super El Nino through ChatGPT, your use is also subject to the policies and settings of the service that provides your ChatGPT account. The plugin can use the Super El Nino connection to perform the request you make; it should not be used for emergencies.

Questions or removal requests

Use the project’s support channel for non-sensitive questions. Do not post private details in a public issue.
